## Problem

Determine whether every finite magma satisfying

```text
E677: x = y * (x * ((y*x)*y))
```

also satisfies

```text
E255: x = ((x*x)*x)*x.
```

For each `a`, put

```text
sigma(a)=(a*a)*a,
D(a)=sigma(a)*a=((a*a)*a)*a.
```

The unique-fixer lemma gives

```text
E255 at a
<=> some r satisfies r*a=a
<=> sigma(a)*a=a
<=> D(a)=a.
```

Thus the theorem is exactly `Fix(D)=M`.
